01 / OVERVIEW

Mapping the Moon. Reaching further.

Chandrayaan is a series of lunar missions by ISRO. Together, Chandrayaan-1, Chandrayaan-2 and Chandrayaan-3 have helped India map the Moon, study its mineralogy and demonstrate soft-landing capabilities near the lunar south pole.

02 / MISSION TIMELINE

Three missions. One journey.

01
2008

CHANDRAYAAN-1

India's first lunar mission.

India's first lunar mission. Discovered water molecules on the Moon's surface using the Moon Mineralogy Mapper and other instruments.

02
2019

CHANDRAYAAN-2

Orbiter, lander and rover.

Included an orbiter, lander Vikram and rover Pragyan. The orbiter continues to provide valuable observations of the lunar surface.

03
2023

CHANDRAYAAN-3

A historic soft landing.

Successfully demonstrated a soft landing near the lunar south polar region and deployed the Pragyan rover.

03 / MISSION TECHNOLOGY
01

Launch Vehicles

Chandrayaan missions used India's PSLV, GSLV MkII and LVM3 launch vehicles across different missions.

02

Lunar Science

The missions investigated lunar surface composition, mineralogy, topography and the presence of water.

03

Exploration

Chandrayaan-3 demonstrated India's capability to perform a controlled soft landing and rover operations.
