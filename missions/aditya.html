01 / OVERVIEW

Watching the Sun. Understanding space.

Aditya-L1 is India's first space-based observatory dedicated to studying the Sun. Positioned around the Sun-Earth L1 point, the mission enables continuous observation of solar activity and its effects on space weather.

02 / MISSION TIMELINE

From Earth toward the Sun.

01
2023

LAUNCH

Beginning the solar journey.

Aditya-L1 was launched on 2 September 2023 aboard PSLV-C57 from Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota.

02
2023 — 24

TRANSFER

Travelling toward L1.

The spacecraft performed a series of orbital manoeuvres to gradually increase its orbit before beginning its journey toward the L1 region.

03
2024

L1 HALO ORBIT

Reaching its solar vantage point.

Aditya-L1 was inserted into its intended halo orbit around the Sun-Earth L1 point, enabling continuous solar observation.

03 / SOLAR SCIENCE
01

Solar Corona

Studying the Sun's outer atmosphere and understanding its heating and dynamic behaviour.

02

Solar Wind

Observing particles and processes associated with the continuous flow of solar material into space.

03

Space Weather

Studying solar eruptions and their influence on the near-Earth space environment.
