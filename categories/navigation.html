01 / OVERVIEW

Finding your way from space.

NavIC, or Navigation with Indian Constellation, provides navigation, positioning and timing services for civilian and strategic applications across India and the surrounding region.

02 / SYSTEM

A constellation built for India.

01

IRNSS Series

The earlier IRNSS spacecraft formed the foundation of India's regional navigation system.

02

Multiple Bands

NavIC services use multiple signal bands to support positioning and timing applications.

03

Regional Coverage

The system is designed around India and surrounding regions.
