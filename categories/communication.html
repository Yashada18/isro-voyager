01 / OVERVIEW

Connecting India from orbit.

Communication satellites form an important part of India's space infrastructure. They provide satellite-based communication services across large geographical regions.

These systems support telecommunications, television broadcasting, satellite news gathering, broadband connectivity and other communication applications.

02 / KEY SYSTEMS

India's orbital communication network.

01

INSAT Series

A multi-purpose satellite system supporting telecommunications, television broadcasting, meteorological services and other applications.

02

GSAT Series

Geosynchronous communication satellites developed to support India's growing communication and broadcasting requirements.

03

GSAT-20

A high-throughput communication satellite designed to expand broadband connectivity and satellite communication capabilities.

04

GSAT-24

A communication satellite designed to provide Ku-band communication services over India.

05

INSAT-3D / 3DR

Multi-purpose satellites that combine meteorological observations with communication and related services.
